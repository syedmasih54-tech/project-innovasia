Benefit vs. Share of Cross-Bank Rings

Recall across topologies as rings span across banks (0.0 → 1.0)

At 0% cross-bank rings, isolated and shared modes perform equally (benefit ≈ 0). As cross-bank rings increase to 100%, isolated detection drops from 95.8% to 79.2%, while shared modes remain resilient at 95.8%–100.0%.

Detection Power vs. Sync Delay

Effect of propagation latency (0s, 30s, 60s, 300s) on detection recall

At 300s (5-minute) delay, simple flag sharing recall drops from 91.7% to 87.5% as adversaries rapidly move money before tokens sync. Shared counts remains 100% resilient across all delays.
Signal Sharing Data Governance

Privacy Boundary & Cryptographic Verification

Strict institutional data boundaries enforced in code for all inter-bank signal transmission

Exactly What is Shared
  • • token: HMAC-SHA256(consortium_secret, account_id)
  • • bank_id: Publishing institution identifier (e.g. "Bank B")
  • • flag_time: Timestamp when local HOLD or STEP-UP occurred
  • • ttl: Time-to-live for cache expiration (default 24 hours)
  • • counts: Token-level distinct sender count in last 24h (optional)
What is Never Shared
  • ✗ raw account id: Account numbers, UPI VPAs, IFSC, mobile numbers
  • ✗ amount: Rupee transaction amounts, account balances, velocity ₹
  • ✗ name: Customer full names, KYC documentation, addresses
  • ✗ true labels: Flags originate strictly from bank's own policy rules
Important Architectural Notice

Tokens are pseudonymised, not anonymous. A real deployment needs key management and legal agreements.
